# Pell

## Current state

A gaunt man in a threadbare grey coat. He is an independent logistics broker who knows of Venn's debt to Oskar Brandt. He is currently leaving the Ruie Highport Bar.

## History

### Scene 1: [[scenes/001-the-grey-coat-broker]]

Approached Venn at the bar. Offered a 50,000-credit job to haul sealed containers to Varo Station via Kessel. Rejected Venn's counter-offer to change the destination to Regina for 12,000 credits. Left the bar without concluding a deal.
