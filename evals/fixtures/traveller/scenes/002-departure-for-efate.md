# Scene 2: Departure for Efate

Kara Venn returned to the Wandering Star and attempted to initiate a cargo run to Regina, but Dex and Mira Sato confirmed there was no manifest, no cargo, and no finalized deal with Pell. When Venn insisted on jumping to Regina, Mira Sato revealed the star was not in their nav plot and could not be plotted without coordinates. Venn abandoned the Regina plan and ordered a jump to Efate to seek honest cargo. The Wandering Star successfully jumped to the Efate system, arriving with 60% fuel and an empty hold.
