# Scene 1: The Grey Coat Broker

Kara Venn waited in the Ruie Highport Bar for Oskar Brandt's collectors, but a black sedan parked outside and a broker named Pell approached instead. Pell offered a 50,000-credit job to haul sealed containers to Varo Station via Kessel, explicitly referencing Venn's debt to Brandt. Venn attempted to renegotiate the route to Regina for 12,000 credits, which Pell rejected with hostility. Pell left the bar without a deal, leaving the offer open but the broker gone.
