# Test Traveller

## Premise

Kara Venn is an ex-scout and current captain of the battered free trader *Wandering Star*. She commands a crew of two: Dex, the engineer, and Mira Sato, the pilot. Venn owes 40,000 credits to Oskar Brandt, a Ruie loan shark. The campaign began in the starport bar at Ruie Highport, where Venn sought to resolve her financial obligations.

## Party and Ship

**Kara Venn:** Captain. Former scout. Primary decision-maker.
**Dex:** Engineer. Responsible for ship maintenance and technical systems.
**Mira Sato:** Pilot. Responsible for navigation and flight operations.
**Wandering Star:** Battered free trader. Current status: 60% fuel, empty cargo hold.

## Situation

As of the end of the latest scene, the *Wandering Star* has successfully jumped to the Efate system. The ship arrived with 60% fuel and an empty hold. Venn initially attempted to initiate a cargo run to Regina based on a partial offer from a broker named Pell. However, Dex and Mira Sato confirmed there was no manifest, no cargo, and no finalized deal with Pell. Furthermore, Mira Sato revealed that Regina was not in their nav plot and could not be plotted without specific coordinates. Venn abandoned the Regina plan and ordered a jump to Efate to seek honest cargo. The crew is currently in the Efate system, looking for new work.

## Active threads

*   **Debt to Oskar Brandt:** Venn owes 40,000 credits to Oskar Brandt. This debt remains unresolved and is a primary source of pressure.
*   **Pell’s Offer:** A broker named Pell offered a 50,000-credit job to haul sealed containers to Varo Station via Kessel. Venn attempted to renegotiate the route to Regina for 12,000 credits, which Pell rejected with hostility. Pell left the bar without a deal, but the original offer to Varo Station via Kessel remains open.
*   **Nav Plot Limitation:** The *Wandering Star* lacks coordinates for Regina. To travel to Regina, Venn must obtain coordinates or plot the star manually.
*   **Cargo Acquisition:** The ship’s hold is empty. Venn is currently in the Efate system seeking honest cargo to generate income and pay down the debt.
