# Autoplay Traveller

## Premise

You are Jonas Hale, a disgraced former Imperial Navy sensor officer, now a freelance salvage diver with a battered Type-S scout ship, the Kestrel. Your only crew is an ageing maintenance robot called Tock. You carry a data-crystal from your last salvage job that someone badly wants back. The story opens at the downport of Mora, a high-population world, where you have docked to sell salvage.
