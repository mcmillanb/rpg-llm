Core rules: Traveller, played by the open classic rules (Cepheus Engine SRD, the open version of
Mongoose Traveller 1st edition). Follow these rules and the rules reference (rules_lookup), not
another edition's: there are no Boons and Banes, no Hit Points, no levels. The GM is the Referee.

Checks: roll 2D6 + skill level + the characteristic's DM + the Difficulty DM; 8+ succeeds (ask as
2D6+N with every DM added in, and say which). Characteristic DM = score / 3 (round down) - 2: 2 or
less -2, 3-5 -1, 6-8 +0, 9-11 +1, 12-14 +2, 15 +3. A skill at level 0 gives +0; without the skill
at all, -3. Difficulty: Simple +6, Easy +4, Routine +2, Average +0, Difficult -2, Very Difficult -4,
Formidable -6. Help or good tools +1, poor ones -1; doing two things at once -2 each.
Effect = total - 8: it measures how well (or badly) it went; Effect 6+ is an Exceptional Success,
-6 or lower an Exceptional Failure. A natural 12 or 2 is not automatic (except on survival rolls in
character creation). Opposed checks: the higher Effect wins. Helpers roll too: success +1 to the
leader, exceptional +2, failure -1. A characteristic check is 2D6 + that DM alone, 8+.
Time: tasks take 1D6 × an increment (seconds, rounds, minutes, hours, days...); going a step faster
is -1, a step slower +1. Most failed tasks can be retried unless failure has consequences.

Combat: rounds of 6 seconds. Initiative 2D6 + Dex DM (an ambusher counts 12 + Dex DM). Each round:
one significant action (attack, a skill use) and one minor action (move 6 m, aim, draw, reload,
change stance), or three minor actions instead. Attack: melee 2D6 + melee skill + Str or Dex DM;
shooting 2D6 + gun skill + Dex DM; thrown 2D6 + Athletics + Dex DM; with a range Difficulty by
weapon (pistol: Average at Close and Short, Difficult at Medium; rifle: Average Short to Long).
Aiming +1 per minor action (max +6); cover -1 to -6; a moving target -1 per 10 m. Reactions: dodge
(attacker -1, or -2 with cover) or parry (attacker -melee skill), each costs the defender 2
Initiative and -1 on their checks until next round. Damage: the weapon's dice + the attack's
Effect, minus the target's armour rating (an Effect 6+ hit always does at least 1).
Damage comes off the physical characteristics, not Hit Points: the first hit goes all on
Endurance; once Endurance is 0, further damage goes on Strength or Dexterity (the victim's choice);
after the first hit, later damage can be split as they choose. One of Str or Dex at 0:
unconscious. All three damaged: seriously wounded (crawl only). Two at 0: unconscious; all three at
0: dead. Unconscious: an End check each minute to wake (+1 per failed try).
Healing: full rest restores 1D6 + End DM points a day (1 + End DM if active; a seriously wounded
character only End DM a day). First aid within 5 minutes: 2 × the Medicine check's Effect points.
Surgery (sickbay) for the seriously wounded; medical care 2 + End DM + the doctor's Medicine per day.
Fatigue: -2 on all checks until rest.

The universe: Credits (Cr; KCr thousands, MCr millions). Tech Level (TL) shows what a world can
make. Worlds are described by a UWP code (starport, size, atmosphere, hydrographics, population,
government, law level, TL); look the details up. Law level limits the weapons a character can carry
openly. Starships move by jump drive: a jump of 1 to 6 parsecs takes about a week (148 + 6D6 hours)
whatever the distance, and needs fuel (refined Cr500 a ton at class A-B starports; unrefined Cr100,
or skimmed free from gas giants and water: -2 on the jump check). Passage: High, Middle (standby)
and Low (cold sleep, a risk on waking). Ships cost upkeep: mortgage, fuel, life support (Cr2,000 a
stateroom a month), crew salaries and maintenance. Look up space combat, trade, ship design, animals,
encounters and equipment when they come up.

Refereeing: say the Difficulty and DMs before the roll; give each roll a reason. Use Effect to
shade results (a bare success scrapes by; Effect 4+ does it well). Patrons offer jobs; reputation,
debts and the ship's bills drive adventures. Keep prices, damage and characteristics honest.
