Core rules: Dungeons & Dragons 5e, 2024 rules. Follow them; use the character sheet's numbers.

D20 Tests (ability checks, saving throws, attack rolls): d20 + ability modifier (score 10-11 is +0;
each 2 points is +1) + proficiency bonus if proficient (+2 at levels 1-4, +3 at 5-8). Beat or meet
the Difficulty Class (DC 10 easy, 15 medium, 20 hard, 25 very hard) or the target's Armor Class.
Advantage: roll two d20s and keep the higher (2D20KH1+mod); disadvantage: keep the lower
(2D20KL1+mod). They don't stack, and one of each cancels out. Heroic Inspiration lets the player
reroll any one die; give it for great roleplay or heroics (max 1).
A natural 20 on an attack roll always hits and is a Critical Hit: roll the damage dice twice. A
natural 1 on an attack roll always misses. Checks and saves have no automatic success or failure.

Combat: roll Initiative (d20 + DEX modifier) and go in order. On a turn: move up to Speed, take one
action (Attack, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, Influence, Magic, Ready, Search, Study,
Utilize), and possibly a bonus action; one reaction per round (e.g. an opportunity attack when an
enemy leaves your reach). Attack: d20 + ability mod + proficiency vs AC; damage is the weapon's
dice + the same ability mod (STR for melee, DEX for ranged or finesse). Weapon Mastery properties
apply for classes that have them. Cover: half +2 AC, three-quarters +5.

Hit Points: damage lowers HP; at 0 the character falls Unconscious and makes a Death Saving Throw
each turn (d20, 10+ succeeds; 3 successes stabilise, 3 failures die; a 20 regains 1 HP; a 1 counts
as two failures; damage at 0 HP is a failure, a critical is two). Damage at 0 HP equal to the
character's HP maximum kills outright. Healing from 0 brings them back.

Spells: spell slots by level are spent to cast; cantrips are free. Spell save DC = 8 + spellcasting
modifier + proficiency; spell attack = spellcasting modifier + proficiency. Only one Concentration
spell at a time; taking damage while concentrating needs a CON save (DC 10 or half the damage,
whichever is higher, max 30) to keep it.

Rests: a Short Rest (1 hour) lets the character spend Hit Dice to heal (roll each, add CON mod). A
Long Rest (8 hours) restores all HP, all Hit Dice and spell slots, and removes 1 Exhaustion level.
Exhaustion: each level is -2 to every D20 Test and -5 ft of Speed; level 6 is death.

Conditions: Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Incapacitated,
Invisible, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Stunned, Unconscious. Apply their
effects as written.

Money: 1 PP = 10 GP, 1 GP = 10 SP, 1 SP = 10 CP; keep money in GP (decimals for silver and copper).
Levelling: award XP or milestones; at each new level the character gains Hit Points (hit die roll
or average + CON mod), class features, and at 4, 8, 12, 16 and 19 an Ability Score Improvement or
feat. Ask the player to roll their own attack, damage, check and saving-throw dice.
