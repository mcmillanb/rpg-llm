**Elf**: Medium (about 5–6 feet tall); speed 30 feet; traits: Darkvision, Elven Lineage, Fey Ancestry, Keen Senses, Trance

**Halfling**: Small (about 2–3 feet tall); speed 30 feet; traits: Brave, Halfling Nimbleness, Luck, Naturally Stealthy
**Human**: Medium (about 4–7 feet tall) or Small (about 2–4 feet tall), chosen when you select this species; speed 30 feet; traits: Resourceful, Skillful, Versatile
**Orc**: Medium (about 6–7 feet tall); speed 30 feet; traits: Adrenaline Rush, Darkvision, Relentless Endurance
**Tiefling**: Medium (about 4–7 feet tall) or Small (about 3–4 feet tall), chosen when you select this species; speed 30 feet; traits: Darkvision, Fiendish Legacy, Otherworldly Presence
